# Privacy Policy for Word Hunt: Word Search Adventure

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026

**Contact Email:** skistudio2007@gmail.com  

---

## 1. Introduction
Word Hunt: Word Search Adventure ("we", "our", or "the Game") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, usage, and disclosure when you use our Windows desktop and web applications.

---

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
Word Hunt is engineered as a 100% offline-first application:
- **No Personal Information:** We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or physical locations.
- **No Device Identifiers:** We do not collect or track advertising IDs, MAC addresses, IMEI numbers, or hardware serials.
- **No Behavioral Tracking:** We do not track user behavior, search terms, or keystrokes outside of the local game session.
- **No Biometrics or Contacts:** We do not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or photo gallery.

---

## 3. Local Storage & Data Retention
All gameplay data — including completed levels, earned stars, unlocked worlds, high scores, sound preferences, and achievements — is stored strictly locally on your own device using standard local browser/app storage.
- This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any remote servers.
- You can reset or delete this data at any time by clearing the application data or using the reset option in game settings.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Family Friendly Policy)
Word Hunt is rated for all ages (PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone) and strictly complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or any user of any age).
- The game contains zero third-party behavioral advertisements, zero social media integrations, and zero chat functions.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services & Analytics
The application does not include any third-party tracking scripts, analytics SDKs (such as Google Analytics or Facebook SDK), or ad networks. The application functions completely independently of external network calls.

---

## 6. Security
Because all application state and progress are kept exclusively on your local device, your data is protected by your operating system's built-in sandbox security mechanisms.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published in this document and accessible through the game's settings menu.
